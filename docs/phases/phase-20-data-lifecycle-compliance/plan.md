# Phase 20 Plan: Data Lifecycle, Compliance, And Trust

## Goal

Make customer security reviews answerable with implemented controls and clear
limitations.

## Scope

- Data deletion by workspace, source, and user.
- Retention policy controls.
- Export support.
- Secret rotation.
- Security review artifacts.
- Privacy/security docs.
- DPA/SOC2-ready control mapping.
- Incident/runbook process.
- Abuse and rate-limit protection.

## Non-Goals

- No claim of SOC 2 certification before certification.
- No custom legal workflow.
- No unsupported promise of provider-side deletion beyond what providers expose.

## Exit Criteria

- Deletion/export/retention jobs are auditable and testable.
- Secret rotation paths exist for supported secrets.
- Security review answers map to controls or known limitations.
