# Phase 22 Autoplan Review

## Verdict

Proceed as a gate, not a build phase. Phase 22 exists to prevent calling the
product enterprise-ready before the evidence supports it.

## CEO Review

Mode: hold scope.

The business risk is over-selling readiness. The useful output is a clear launch
decision: ready, invite-only, blocked, or ready with named limitations.

## Design Review

Customer-facing limitation docs must be plain and specific. Do not hide missing
enterprise features behind vague language.

## Engineering Review

Use evidence from prior phases. If a control was not tested or drilled, mark it
as a blocker or limitation.

## Decision Log

- Phase 22 is a launch gate.
- Known limitations are a required artifact.
- Security and support reviews are blocking inputs.

## Approval Conditions

- Cross-phase evidence exists.
- Blockers have owners.
- Customer-facing readiness claims match product behavior.
